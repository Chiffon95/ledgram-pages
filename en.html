MAKE IT YOURS

Make it look your way.

A closer look at the features documented in our README.

01 / COLORS & STYLE

Colors, gradients, and text styles

Choose solid text and background colors, six static or four animated text gradients, and bold, italic, or underlined text. Adjust gradient animation speed independently.

02 / MOTION & EFFECTS

Motion on your terms

Keep text still or move it in either direction. Add Blink, Pulse, or Fade effects, with independent controls for scrolling and effect speeds.

03 / PRESETS

Save what you like

Keep your favorite setups in the local preset library, complete with saved previews. Load, rename, overwrite, delete, or undo a preset load.

FULL-SCREEN DISPLAY

Let the message take over.

Switch between Text + Glow and Dot Matrix styles. The full-screen Display shows your message without ads, with orientation following and Lock / Unlock on phones, tablets, and foldables.
